What is MNT staking

What MNT staking is

Staking the Mantle gas token

MNT staking means committing your MNT, the gas token of the Mantle network, to a staking contract in return for staking rewards. While the tokens are staked they are locked or restricted in some way, and rewards build up for as long as they stay committed.

Why people stake

Holding MNT in a wallet earns nothing by itself. Staking puts the same tokens to work, so holders who plan to keep MNT for the long term can receive a reward on top of their balance. The size of the reward depends on the staking program and is not fixed by this page.

Stake vs hold

Staking compared with holding

Staking and plain holding both leave you exposed to the MNT price. The difference is what you give up in return for a reward.

In practice

Stake only the share of MNT you do not expect to need soon, and check the unstaking terms before you commit.

How to stake

How to stake MNT

Review, then sign

Staking is a set of on-chain transactions that you approve one by one. Nothing is staked until you sign.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and make sure it is on the Mantle network.
  2. Enter how much MNT to stake and review the terms shown.
  3. Approve the token if the staking contract asks for an allowance.
  4. Sign the stake transaction and wait for confirmation.
  5. Return later to claim rewards or start unstaking, depending on the program.

Reward accrual and any lock or wait period follow the rules of the staking contract, so read them before you confirm.

Fees & gas

What MNT staking costs

Gas in MNT

Every action is a transaction on Mantle, and gas there is paid in MNT. Read the Mantle docs for how the network works.

Program fees

A staking program may keep a share of rewards or charge a fee on unstaking. Any such cost should be visible in the terms before you sign, and this page does not set it.

Keep MNT for gas

Do not stake your entire balance. Leave some MNT unstaked so you can still pay gas to claim rewards or unstake later.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface prepares transactions; it never holds your MNT or your keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the staking contract address and the network before approving.
  • Approve only what you need and revoke old allowances you no longer use.
  • Read the lock and unstaking terms so you know when you can access the tokens again.
  • Staking contracts can hold bugs and rewards can change, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

MNT Staking FAQ

What is MNT staking?

MNT staking means committing MNT, the gas token of Mantle, to a staking contract to earn rewards. The tokens are locked or restricted while staked. Rewards accrue for as long as they stay committed.

How do I earn rewards by staking MNT?

You stake MNT from your own wallet and rewards build up over time under the rules of the staking program. You can then claim them or leave them to keep accruing, depending on how the program works. This page does not promise a reward rate.

Can I unstake my MNT at any time?

That depends on the program. Some staking contracts apply a lock or a waiting period before tokens are released, so check the terms before you stake. Gas is needed to start and to finish the process.

Does the interface hold my MNT?

No. You connect your own wallet and sign each transaction, and the interface never takes custody of funds or keys. The tokens sit in the staking contract you approve, not with this site.

Which network do I need to stake MNT on?

MNT is the gas token on Mantle, so you need your wallet connected to the Mantle network. Keep some MNT unstaked for gas fees. Check the network name before you sign.

Is MNT staking risk free?

No. The MNT price can change, staking contracts can contain bugs, and terms or rewards can be updated by the program. Stake only an amount you can afford to have locked.
